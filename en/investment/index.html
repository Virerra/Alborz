About Us

Alborz Investment

Part of Alborz Industrial Group — since 1346 (1967)

Alborz Investment is the newest branch of Alborz Industrial Group — a bridge between the family's industrial experience and today's investment opportunities.

This branch is active in three areas: financing construction projects, partnering in the launch and growth of various businesses, and industrial investment. With an office in Bandar Abbas and an office in Istanbul, Alborz Investment follows the same careful, long-term outlook that has built Alborz Industrial Group over more than half a century.

  • Construction project financing
  • Business launch partnerships
  • Industrial investment
  • Active in Bandar Abbas and Istanbul
