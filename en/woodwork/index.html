About Us

Kabinet Alborz

Part of Alborz Industrial Group — since 1346 (1967)

Kabinet Alborz is the cabinetry and interior-decoration branch of Alborz Industrial Group, based in Bandar Abbas.

This branch focuses on the design and execution of kitchen cabinets, MDF and PVC work, interior decoration and decorative mirrors — carrying the same standard that has guided the Alborz family for more than half a century: precision in execution, and quality that speaks for itself.

  • Custom design for every space
  • Execution in wood, MDF and PVC
  • Interior decoration matched to the cabinetry
  • Custom decorative mirrors
