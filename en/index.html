The Name

"Alborz" was founded by Heydar Samimi in 1346 (1967), in Bandar Abbas — named for the mountain range whose peaks, Damavand among them, have always stood linked together; just as he hoped his own family would carry this work forward, generation after generation, side by side.

This company was never born out of financial necessity; it grew from its founder's genuine love of the craft. The work began with the manufacture of iron doors and windows, and over more than half a century it grew into a group with three independent branches: Woodwork, Metalwork, and Investment — each carrying the same founding outlook.
