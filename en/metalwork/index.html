About Us

Alborz Metalwork

Part of Alborz Industrial Group — the group's first activity, since 1346 (1967)

Alborz Metalwork — known locally in Bandar Abbas as "Alborz Ironworks" — is the very trade Alborz Industrial Group began with: the manufacture of iron doors and windows.

Heydar Samimi, the group's founder, started out alone, making iron doors and windows in Bandar Abbas. As the city grew, so did this branch, becoming one of the first organizers of Bandar Abbas's metalworking tradespeople. Years later, aluminum and UPVC were added to its work. Today, Alborz Metalwork has carried out nearly all of Hormozgan province's public and government projects in this field.

  • Iron, aluminum and UPVC doors & windows
  • Metal structures for large projects
  • Experience with public and government projects
  • More than half a century of track record
